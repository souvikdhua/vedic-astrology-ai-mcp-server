# The interpretive technique

`interpretation.md` covers *presentation* (how to lay out a reading). This file
covers the *method* — the repeatable Parashari algorithm for turning computed
chart data into a synthesized reading. Follow it in order; a reading is a
**weighted synthesis of multiple testimonies**, never a per-field data dump.

The golden rule: **a theme is only strong if it shows from several independent
references.** One placement is a hint; three converging testimonies are a
conclusion.

---

## Step 0 — Set the frame
Fix the ayanamsa (default **Lahiri**; the cloned app used Raman — say which).
Lay out the D1 (Rasi) chart, the Moon as an alternate ascendant (Chandra
lagna), and at least the D9 (Navamsa). Note birth context: day/night, paksha
(waxing/waning — a New-Moon Moon is weak in *paksha bala*).

## Step 1 — The three pillars (read all three, cross-confirm)
- **Lagna** = body, vitality, life direction. Judge the rising sign **and** the
  **Lagnesha** (lagna lord): where it sits, its dignity, the house it rules into.
- **Moon (Chandra lagna)** = mind/emotions. Re-read the whole chart counting
  houses *from the Moon*; themes appearing from both Lagna and Moon are real.
- **Sun** = soul, authority, father, recognition.

## Step 2 — Find the chart's centre of gravity
The **most-tenanted house / strongest stellium** usually sets the life's
dominant theme. Name it first; everything else orbits it.

## Step 3 — Bhava analysis by the three-fold method
For any life area, triangulate the **house** three ways and combine:
1. **Karaka** — the natural significator planet (e.g. Jupiter→wealth/children,
   Venus→spouse, Sun→father, Saturn→career/longevity, Moon→mother/mind).
2. **Bhava** — the house itself: occupants + planets aspecting it.
3. **Bhavesha** — the house *lord's* placement, dignity, and destination house.

Convert house numbers to significations: 1 self/body · 2 wealth/speech/family ·
3 courage/siblings/skill · 4 home/mother/heart · 5 intellect/children/purva-punya
· 6 enemies/debt/service/health · 7 partnership/marriage · 8 transformation/
longevity/occult · 9 dharma/fortune/father/higher-learning · 10 career/status ·
11 gains/network · 12 loss/expense/foreign/moksha.

## Step 4 — Strength & state (does the promise get delivered?)
A placement only *delivers* if the significator is strong. Check:
dignity (exalt / own / friend / neutral / enemy / debil), retrogradation,
combustion, **digbala** (directional strength), and **Ashtakavarga** bindus of
the sign as a numeric cross-check. Strong + well-placed = promise kept; weak or
afflicted = promise diluted or delayed.

## Step 5 — Functional nature (for THIS lagna)
Classify each planet for the given ascendant as functional benefic / malefic,
**yogakaraka** (rules a kendra *and* a trikona), **maraka** (2nd/7th lords), or
**badhaka**. This is what decides whether a planet's *dasha* helps or hurts —
the same planet is benefic for one lagna and malefic for another.

## Step 6 — Yogas and doshas (amplifiers / modifiers)
Detect named combinations and let them raise or lower the baseline:
Raja yogas (kendra+trikona lord links), Dhana yogas (2/5/9/11 links),
Pancha Mahapurusha, Budha-Aditya, Gajakesari, Neecha-bhanga, etc.; and doshas —
Kuja/Mangal (Mars in 1/2/4/7/8/12), Kemadruma, Sade Sati. State the rule that
fires, not just the name.

## Step 7 — Confirm in the vargas
Re-test specifics in the relevant divisional chart: **D9** for marriage, dharma,
and planetary "ripening" (a planet **vargottama** — same sign in D1 and D9 — is
notably strengthened); **D10** for career, and so on. Vargas confirm or veto D1
promises.

## Step 8 — Timing: Dasha + Gochara
The **Vimshottari dasha** activates parts of the chart in sequence. For each
maha/antar lord, read it by its **natal placement + functional lordship + karaka
role** — that triad says what the period delivers. Overlay **transits** (Gochara),
especially Saturn/Jupiter and any Sade Sati. The Jaimini **chara karakas**
(esp. Atmakaraka = soul, Darakaraka = spouse) add a parallel timing/theme layer.

## Step 9 — Synthesize and weight
Now combine, don't list. Heuristics:
- Repeated themes across Lagna / Moon / karaka / lord dominate.
- The **Lagnesha** and the **centre-of-gravity house** define the core self.
- The **Atmakaraka** + the **current dasha** tell you *what is live now* — lead
  the "present" part of the reading with these.
- Resolve contradictions by **strength and number of testimonies**, not by
  picking the more dramatic one.
- Group output by life area; lead with the strongest, best-supported signal.

## Step 10 — Frame responsibly
Probabilistic and agency-respecting, never deterministic. Handle health,
longevity, marriage, and finance with extra care; this is offered for reflection,
not as fact or professional advice. State the ayanamsa used.
